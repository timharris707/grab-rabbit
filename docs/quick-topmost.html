Quick Topmost

One hotkey records the window in front of you. While it records, two surfaces say so: a pill on the window and an indicator in the menu bar.

On the window

The pill appears under the title bar of the window being recorded. It is never part of the recording.

In the menu bar

The indicator stays for the whole recording, even when Grab Rabbit has no menu bar icon otherwise.
